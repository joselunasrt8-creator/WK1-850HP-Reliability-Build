# Architecture

## Locked Route A

**Hellcat/Redeye 6.2 → built NAG1/W5A580 → NV146 → WK1 3.73 front/rear differentials → upgraded shafts/axles/CVs only where justified**

## Why this architecture

Route A preserves more of the WK1's native transmission position, crossmember logic, transfer-case location, differential geometry, axle ratio, and potentially portions of the original shaft architecture.

This reduces custom fabrication and avoids making the build dependent on unresolved newer-generation AWD network integration.

## Reliability philosophy

The build is not designed around unrestricted drag-race launches. It is designed around:

- ~800–850 WHP
- hard street pulls
- occasional controlled launches
- drag-radial use
- hot-weather operation
- long-duration street driving
- retained A/C and normal street manners

The main engineering risk is **shock load**, not simply horsepower.

## Torque-management policy

- Preserve torque reduction during every WOT shift.
- Limit first-gear delivered torque during commissioning.
- Avoid deleting factory/TCM torque intervention for the sake of dyno or ET numbers.
- Avoid two-step/transbrake-style repeated launches.
- Treat tire grip as a driveline load multiplier.

## Native WK1 systems intentionally retained initially

- 3.73 final-drive ratio
- front differential architecture
- rear 226 mm / Super 44 housing and gearset
- front CV architecture
- hubs unless condition requires replacement
- transmission/crossmember placement logic
- NV146 placement
- compliant suspension bushings unless wheel hop is demonstrated

## Systems intentionally overbuilt

- NAG1 internal capacity
- NV146 durability package
- front/rear driveshaft capacity
- rear axle shafts
- transmission cooling

## Operating-envelope boundary

Inside intended envelope:

- street tires / drag radials
- controlled brake-boost launches
- repeated roll-on acceleration
- repeated high-load pulls with thermal control

Outside intended envelope:

- repeated slick launches
- two-step abuse
- transbrake use
- repeated prepared-surface launches without cooldown
- calibration that removes shift torque reduction
