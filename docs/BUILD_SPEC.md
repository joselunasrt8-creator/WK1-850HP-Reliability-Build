# 850-WHP Drivetrain Specification

## Powertrain

**Engine:** Hellcat/Redeye 6.2L supercharged HEMI

Target output: approximately **800–850 WHP** with reliability prioritized over peak output.

## Transmission — NAG1/W5A580

Recommended specification: **max-effort AWD NAG1 hardware package**, independent of vendor naming.

Required features to validate with builder:

- 300M input shaft
- new / upgraded output shaft as appropriate
- increased K1/K2/K3/B2 clutch capacity
- performance frictions and steels
- heavy-duty F1/F2 sprags
- high-pressure tested / calibrated valve body
- remanufactured or fully inspected pump
- new conductor plate, seals, filter and service hardware
- documented planetary inspection
- preserved torque-management compatibility

### Converter

Target: **9.5-inch multi-disc lockup**, approximately **2,800-rpm stall**.

Avoid unnecessary high stall because of heat and launch shock.

### Flexplate

SFI-rated flexplate appropriate to the selected 6.2/NAG1 interface, with new high-grade/ARP fasteners where compatible.

### Transmission cooling

- large stacked-plate external cooler
- high-capacity pan if clearance allows
- controlled thermal bypass
- -6AN or appropriately sized lines
- dedicated temperature logging

Working target:

- preferred: **170–190°F**
- derate around **195°F**
- stop repeated launches/pulls by approximately **205°F** pending builder/fluid guidance

## Transfer case — NV146

Recommended: **proactively built / max-effort NV146** before enabling full power.

Specification to validate with builder:

- HD chain/bearings as applicable
- upgraded clutch pack / steels
- pump inspection/rebuild
- complete seals/bearings service
- verified actuator/control health

A healthy stock NV146 may be used only for low-power shakedown if desired; it is not the final 850-WHP specification.

## Driveshafts

### Front

Heavy-duty front shaft sized by the shaft manufacturer after installed geometry is frozen.

### Rear

Target baseline: **4-inch × 0.125-inch-wall aluminum** or equivalent vendor-approved solution.

No shaft should be ordered until final ride height, flange faces, working angles, plunge requirements, and critical-speed requirements are measured.

## Front differential / CVs

- retain the stock-size 200 mm front differential initially
- inspect / refresh before full-power commissioning
- retain OE-quality front CV architecture initially
- replace hubs/CVs based on condition
- custom front axles only after demonstrated need

## Rear differential / axles

- retain and refresh the stock 226 mm / Super 44 housing and 3.73 gearset
- install rear Wavetrac LSD
- install heavy-duty rear axle shafts
- inspect pinion/ring-gear condition and setup carefully

## Mounts and bushings

- performance engine mounts around the 80A-poly range if NVH remains acceptable
- moderate-poly transmission mount
- upgraded front-differential bushings/mounts
- retain compliant cradle/control-arm bushings unless wheel hop is documented

## Torque-management strategy

Initial commissioning target:

- first gear: approximately **600–650 lb-ft delivered torque ceiling**
- second gear: approximately **700–750 lb-ft delivered torque ceiling**
- allow full 850-WHP airflow from third gear once the system is validated
- preserve shift torque reduction on every WOT shift

These are initial control targets, not universal calibration values; final torque limits belong to the tuner and transmission builder after logged testing.

## Launch limits

Initial baseline:

- launch approximately **1,800–2,200 rpm**
- approximately **3–5 psi brake boost maximum** during early validation
- drag radials preferred over slicks
- no two-step
- no transbrake
- no repeated prepared-surface back-to-back launches
- keep all four tire rolling circumferences closely matched; target ≤0.5% difference

## Verdict

**YELLOW — build with stated operating limits.**

The architecture is suitable for a reliability-first 800–850 WHP street WK1 when torque delivery, temperature, tire choice, and launch behavior are controlled.
